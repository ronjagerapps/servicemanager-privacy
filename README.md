Service Manager Privacy Policy

Welcome to Service Manager.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the Service Manager application.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Account information
* Services you create
* Customer information entered by you
* Calendar events
* Payments
* Income and expense records
* Notes created by you
* Language preferences
* Country and region settings
* Subscription status
* App settings
* Device information necessary for security and app functionality

AI Assistant

If you use the AI Assistant, your questions may be processed by third-party AI providers to generate responses.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in AI conversations.

Advertising

The Free version of Service Manager may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its partners may collect information such as:

* Advertising identifiers
* Device information
* Approximate location
* Ad interaction information

Advertising is managed according to Google’s policies.

Users may be asked for advertising consent where required by law.

Premium and Business subscribers do not see advertisements.

Subscription Information

The application offers Free, Premium, and Business plans.

Subscription processing may be handled through Google Play.

We do not receive your full payment card information.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

* Provide application features
* Manage appointments
* Manage customers
* Track income and expenses
* Generate reports
* Synchronize your data
* Improve application performance
* Maintain security
* Provide AI features
* Deliver notifications
* Manage subscriptions
* Display advertisements in the Free version

Data Storage

Your data may be securely stored using trusted cloud services.

Reasonable security measures are used to protect your information.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only with trusted service providers that help operate the application, including:

* Authentication services

* Cloud hosting providers

* Google AdMob

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

* Access your data
* Correct your data
* Delete your data
* Export your data
* Withdraw advertising consent where applicable

Children’s Privacy

Service Manager is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure.
