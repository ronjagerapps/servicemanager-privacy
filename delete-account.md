# Delete Account - Service Manager

Users can permanently delete their Service Manager account at any time.

## How to delete your account

1. Open the Service Manager app.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Go to Settings.
4. Tap "Delete Account".
5. Confirm the deletion.

## What happens after deletion

When your account is deleted:
- Your account is permanently removed.
- Your personal information is deleted.
- Your customers, services, calendar events, payments, reports, and other app data are deleted.
- Data required by law may be retained only if legally necessary.

If you experience any issues deleting your account, contact support through the contact information provided in the app.
